Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2, 2026
Privacy Commitment Summary: Katib is built with zero-trust privacy architecture. We do NOT record, upload, store, or transmit your microphone recordings, system audio, or transcribed text to any remote cloud servers. Speech recognition runs 100% locally on your Mac hardware.

1. Overview and Operator Identity

This Privacy Policy explains how Katib ("we", "us", "our"), operated by Luo Lin (individual operator), handles information when you use our website (https://linrol.eu.org/katib/) and macOS application (Katib).

For inquiries or support, contact us at: linrolmail@gmail.com.

2. Data Collection and Usage

A. macOS Application

  • On-Device Speech Processing: Katib captures audio through macOS ScreenCaptureKit and your microphone strictly for real-time transcription on your local device. Inference is performed entirely on your Apple Silicon / Intel CPU using the open-source SenseVoice model. Audio data is discarded from memory immediately after decoding.
  • No Audio Cloud Upload: Your voice and system audio never leave your device. We operate zero cloud transcription backends.
  • Local Subtitle History: If you choose to export transcripts or save session notes, files are saved locally on your device. You maintain full ownership and control of your files.
  • No Telemetry / No Tracking: Katib does not collect user analytics, tracking cookies, or telemetry identifiers.

B. Website & Storefront

  • Website Analytics: We use privacy-first Cloudflare Web Analytics which does not collect personal identifiers or track across websites.
  • Purchases & Payments: We never collect or store your payment details. All purchases are handled securely by our Merchant of Record, Creem.io, in compliance with PCI-DSS standards.

3. Third-Party Services

  • Creem.io: Our online merchant of record and payment processor (creem.io/privacy).
  • Cloudflare: Provides DNS and SSL infrastructure for our official domain.
